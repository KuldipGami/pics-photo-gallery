# Pics privacy policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

Pics is a photo and video gallery that runs entirely on your computer. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer receives no data from it.

## What stays on your computer

Everything Pics knows about your photos and videos is made and kept on your computer only:

- the list of your photos and videos, their previews, dates, places and camera details;
- **People**: the faces found in your photos and the names you give them (face recognition runs on your computer);
- **search** by what's in a photo and **text in photos** (both run on your computer);
- albums, favorites, ratings, tags, the **Private** marks and PIN, and the History of changes.

None of this is uploaded, shared or sent anywhere. It's stored in your user profile: in the app's own storage when Pics comes from the Microsoft Store (removed when you uninstall it), or in `%APPDATA%\Pics` for the installer and portable versions from GitHub (delete that folder to remove it).

Pics changes your files only when you ask it to (for example: moving duplicates to the Recycle Bin, organizing, saving an edited copy, or writing a rating, tag, date or place into a photo).

## When Pics uses the internet

- **Maps.** While a map is shown (the Map view, Places, or when you set a location), Pics loads map images from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/)'s tile servers. Like any website, those servers receive your IP address and which map squares are shown (and so roughly which area you're looking at). No photos, names or other data are sent. OpenStreetMap's own privacy policy is at <https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy>.
- **Links.** Links you click (for example the license pages in Settings → About) open in your web browser.

Pics doesn't contact any other server. Place names are looked up offline, and the AI models are part of the app.
